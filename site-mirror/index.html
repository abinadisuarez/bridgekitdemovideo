How to use BridgeKit

Three tools that take you from "just installed WP Sync" to "actually getting the promised 3X-5X in commissions." Work through them roughly in this order for a new setup, or jump straight to whichever one you need.

– Before you start

Nothing here needs an account, an API key, or an internet connection after the page loads. Everything you type is saved to your own browser only — see the note at the bottom of this tab.

1 BridgeBlueprint — decide what to build first

WP Sync makes building one bridge easy; the hard part is knowing which ones matter. Use this first.

  1. Open the BridgeBlueprint tab.
  2. Pick your business type from What best describes you?
  3. Check every app you actually use under Which apps do you run?
  4. Read the ranked list on the right and build them inside WP Sync in that order — the highest-priority ones are named first for a reason.

2 FollowUp Forge — write the money-making part

WP Sync moves the lead into your autoresponder. This writes what goes to them once they're there.

  1. Open the FollowUp Forge tab.
  2. Fill in Offer name, Price, and One-line angle.
  3. Pick a Tone.
  4. Click Generate sequence and copy each email into your autoresponder.

3 LeakCalc — see what it's worth

A personalized number for what manual follow-up (or no follow-up) is actually costing you.

  1. Open the LeakCalc tab.
  2. Enter your monthly leads, estimated drop-off rate, and subscriber value.
  3. Click Calculate to see your yearly leak and the 5-year Zapier comparison.

One thing to know

BridgeKit has no accounts, no server, and no monthly bill. Everything you type is saved only in this browser's local storage — nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data clears your saved inputs. Use the same browser next time to pick up where you left off.

FollowUp Forge

The follow-up sequence WP Sync doesn't write for you — Day 2, Day 5, and Day 14, ready to paste into your autoresponder.

What this does: writes a three-email sequence using PAS (Problem — Agitate — Solution), a proven direct-response copywriting structure: Day 2 names the problem, Day 5 makes the cost of waiting concrete, Day 14 closes with the solution and a reason to act now. Why it matters for WP Sync: the bridge gets the lead into your autoresponder the instant it happens — this is what that lead actually reads once they're there, so the automation you built has copy behind it built to convert, not just show up.
